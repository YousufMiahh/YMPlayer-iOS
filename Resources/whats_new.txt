- Subtitles now go to the Apple TV when you AirPlay an MKV film or episode: the same track as on your phone, shown if you had them on (switch them on or off from the Apple TV's subtitle menu). Text subtitles only; picture ones (PGS, VobSub) can't be sent.
- AirPlay now sends the picture to your Apple TV, not just the sound.
- MKV films and episodes can be AirPlayed too: your iPhone repackages them for the Apple TV as you watch (keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV; it can be locked). Skipping, resuming and Skip Intro still work.
- Settings looks like the Android app now: rounded rows with the setting on the right. Tap a row to turn it on or off, or to move to the next choice.
- Choosing a category, a channel, an episode, subtitles, audio, the picture size or a TV now uses the Android app's slide-up panel, with a search box for categories and a heart to favourite them.
